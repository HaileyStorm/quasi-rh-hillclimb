## 2. PROBLEM / NEEDS-EDIT items (most serious first)

1. **Second Euler region hard-codes 7/8 (l.4056 "x_r\ge7/8"; l.5574 "\Re s\ge7/8").** Under sigma0<beta*<=7/8, the principal contours Re s=beta*+e (l.6230, 6253) can have real part below 7/8. Lemma principal-residue-interface then claims "The paths lie in D_2" without justification (l.6232).
   - Fix: replace 7/8 by sigma0, or by any fixed constant <=sigma0 such as 87/100.
   - Why it works: at z_r>=33/200, w_r>=19/20 the binding exponents are 1-x_r-w_r-6z_r (good primes; needs <-1) and 1-x_r-w_r (p|u; needs <0). At x_r=87/100 these are -181/100 and -41/50, so there is ample room.
   - Update l.4173-4174 and l.4184 accordingly; c_H=4/5 survives (tail 81/100).
   - Knock-on (outside this range): Part II's verification that its compensated tuple correction is holomorphic and bounded "in the second Euler region" (l.~8695, 8909, 9052-9070) must be rechecked at x_r>=sigma0. The exponents listed there have margin; for example 4-5x_r-6z_r is about -1.365 at x_r=sigma0.
2. **l.5525/5530: "holomorphic on Re s>7/8", "sup_{Re s>7/8}|H_eta-1|<=1/2".** These must hold on Re s>sigma0, both for the criterion (l.410-413) and for the rightward shift at l.6290-6295, which starts at beta*+e<7/8. The fix follows from item 1. The same text is repeated in Part II at l.15651-15652 ("holomorphic on Re s>7/8").
3. **l.6418-6419 "These paths may be taken in D_1(3/8): ... \Re(s+w)=\beta_*+e+1/2>1+3/8".** This fails for beta*+e<=7/8. Replace with D_1(1/4); nothing else in the proof uses 3/8.
4. **Vestigial hypotheses "Fix sigma0 in [7/8,1) with beta*>sigma0" at l.5811, 6024, 6178, 6345.** Part II invokes them with sigma0=7/8 (l.15532, 15823, 15661). The proofs of fixed-bin-contour and bin-contour-accounting never use sigma0>=7/8. The other two lemmas use it only through items 1 and 3. Restate as sigma0 in (1/2,1) once items 1 and 3 are fixed, and invoke with sigma0=20649/23600.
5. Cross-check for the Part II auditor (outside this range): late-height-closure (and criterion l.402-431) needs |J|<<Z^{C(sigma0)+omega} with omega<beta*-sigma0<=1/23600. Part II states its low bound as Z^{C(7/8)+eps} (l.~15646). C(7/8)=C(sigma0)+1/23600>=C(beta*) when beta*<=7/8, so the contradiction would collapse. The low estimate must therefore deliver exactly C(sigma0)=C(11/12-ell/4) at the new geometry.
