Every box is an exact rational rectangle. The return reserve is \(10^{-9}\). Each final column is an integer \(m>0\) such that every relevant Bernstein coefficient is at least \(m/10^{20}\). They are obtained by taking the exact rational minimum and rounding down after multiplication by \(10^{20}\). The return columns cover all twelve tensor Bernstein coefficients; the admission columns cover the full stated upward admission windows. The formulas in the proof reconstruct the coefficients exactly.
